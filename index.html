ThreadKeep

Folders, search and export for your AI chats

ThreadKeep is a Chrome extension that adds folders and tags to the sidebars of ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Grok, searches everything you have read across all four, and exports any chat to Markdown, PDF, JSON or plain text. Everything stays in your browser. No account.

What it does

Folders on every site

Drag chats into folders, nest them, color them and pin the ones you use most. One folder can hold chats from ChatGPT and Claude side by side.

Search everything

Press Ctrl+Shift+K (Cmd+Shift+K on Mac) to search titles, answers and code from every chat you have opened.

Clean exports

Markdown, PDF, JSON or plain text. Code keeps its language and indentation, tables stay tables.

Private by design

No account, no analytics, no ads. Your folders and saved chats never leave your browser.
