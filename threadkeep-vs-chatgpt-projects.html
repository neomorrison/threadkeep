ThreadKeep vs ChatGPT Projects

These two are often compared because both group chats, but they do different jobs. Projects is a workspace inside ChatGPT. ThreadKeep is a filing layer in your browser.

Where Projects is the better choice

  • You want shared instructions or files used by every chat in a group.
  • You collaborate with other people on the same project.
  • You use ChatGPT on a phone or in the desktop app, where a Chrome extension does not run.
  • You do not want to install anything.

Where ThreadKeep is the better choice

  • You only want to file chats, without changing how ChatGPT answers in them.
  • You need nested folders, tags, colors or pins.
  • You use more than one AI site and want one folder tree and one search box.
  • You want per-chat export to Markdown, PDF, JSON or text.
  • You want your folders kept on your device with no account.

Using both

Nothing stops you from using both. Keep a Project for work that needs shared files and instructions, and file those same chats into ThreadKeep folders by topic or client.

Costs and limits

Free covers up to 5 folders, 10 exports a month and unlimited search. Pro is $4.99 a month or $59 once and removes the folder and export limits.
