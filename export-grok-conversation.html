How to export a Grok chat to Markdown or PDF

We could not find an official per-conversation export for Grok on grok.com. These are the practical options.

Save a Grok conversation

  1. Copy a response. Use the copy icon under a Grok response and paste it into a Markdown file or notes app.
  2. Print to PDF. Open the chat on grok.com, press Ctrl+P (Cmd+P on Mac) and choose Save as PDF. Load the full chat first by scrolling to the top.
  3. Check xAI's help pages. Features change. Look at xAI's help pages for any export or share option before relying on copy and print.
  4. Mind the address. Make sure you are on grok.com when you do this; Grok is also available inside x.com, which behaves differently.

Limits

Copying and printing handle one chat at a time and do not give you JSON. X's account data archive is a different tool and, as far as we know, is not a per-conversation backup.
